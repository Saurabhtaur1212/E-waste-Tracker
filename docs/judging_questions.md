# Likely Judging Questions

1. What exact problem are you solving?
2. Why is segregation at source important?
3. What makes this more than a waste-classification app?
4. How does your AI classify waste?
5. What dataset did you use?
6. What is your confidence threshold?
7. What happens when AI confidence is low?
8. How do you prevent incorrect Green Credit claims?
9. How does QR asset tracking work?
10. How do you prove an e-waste item reached a recycler?
11. How are recycler certificates recorded?
12. How do smart bins communicate?
13. What happens when a bin crosses 90%?
14. How can the system scale to an entire city?
15. What happens without internet connectivity?
16. How would you integrate PostgreSQL/Supabase?
17. How would you protect student and asset data?
18. What are your measurable impact metrics?
19. What is simulated versus actually implemented?
20. What is your business/adoption model?
21. How does the platform support SDG 11?
22. What is your future roadmap?
23. What is technically novel in your solution?
24. How would you validate the AI model in the real world?
25. How would you integrate certified recyclers and municipal systems?
